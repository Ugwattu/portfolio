Parent Portal App

Final-year Android project · school-to-parent communication

A full-featured Android app that gives parents real-time visibility into their child's school life: attendance, grades, fees and more, all in one place. Built end to end for my final year, from the login flow down to the local database.

  • Attendance, report cards & fee vouchers in one dashboard
  • Digital leave requests and a daily diary of classwork
  • Timetable, syllabus & transport details on demand
  • Two-way feedback between parents and school staff
  • Real-time announcements for events and emergencies

FIG. 02

YouTube Sidebar

Browser extension · published on Firefox Add-ons

YouTube crams comments, related videos, playlists and live chat all below the fold. This extension pulls them into a single tabbed sidebar next to the player, plus an A/B video looper with draggable markers for repeating any section of a video.

  • Tabbed sidebar for comments, related, playlist & chat
  • Custom A/B loop with drag-and-drop start/end handles
  • Configurable default tab and button styling
  • Built on Manifest V3
